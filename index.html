About Chiya Pirim

Chiya Pirim is a little tea café in Shankhamul, Baneshwor, made for people who like to slow down. Wooden tables, warm lamps, good food and a pot of tea that is never rushed.

It is a place for quiet mornings, long evening talks, and poetry on Fridays.
